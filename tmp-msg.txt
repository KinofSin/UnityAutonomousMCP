Add per-object cost attribution to the dossier, and correct the toggle-cost claim

New "cost" dossier section: every renderer-holding object with polygons, material
slots, share of total and ifRemoved -- the totals and rank left behind without it
-- sorted by cost, plus an inactive roll-up with ifAllRemoved. Inactive objects
are included deliberately, because VRChat's stats count renderers with
includeInactive, which makes disabled wardrobe toggles the least obvious
candidates.

Rank thresholds now live in one place. PolygonTier/MaterialSlotTier/etc are the
source of truth and PcBudgets is projected from them, so the published JSON shape
is unchanged. "Over" means past the highest tier VRChat publishes for a metric
rather than inventing a name for a tier that does not exist.

Uses GetIndexCount rather than mesh.triangles, which allocates and marshals the
whole index array per renderer -- roughly 150k ints for one head -- only to divide
its length by three. Non-triangle submeshes are skipped instead of miscounted.

Running it corrected two claims recorded earlier today:

- The disabled-toggle cost was quoted in verts, but VRChat ranks polygons and the
  two order differently. ANIMAL DOG is the largest mesh by verts (43,000) and only
  8th by polygons (20,315), so ranking candidates by vertex count picks the wrong
  target.
- The real figure is 86,619 of 278,879 polys (31%), and ifAllRemoved shows that
  deleting every disabled object still leaves 192,260 -- far past the 70,000 good
  ceiling. It buys download size and memory but no rank change, contradicting the
  earlier "beats every importer flag" framing.
